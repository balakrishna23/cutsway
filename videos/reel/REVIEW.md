# REVIEW: Cutsway reel v2 (The Raw Pile, 30 s, 1080 x 1920)

## What changed from v1 (owner feedback, 2026-10-29)
- 15 s to 30 s, faster cuts, more information: four service panels with turnaround (36H / 12H / 48H / 8H, from site-config.js, approved by the owner), 2 rounds of notes, posting to 4 platforms, then the call to action.
- Music: an original 128 BPM house track generated by `../tools/make_music.py rawpile`. Every scene starts on a bar line (1.875 s). Sound effects from the HyperFrames bundled library sit on the razor, whips, numbers, pins and pills.
- The pile is spread on a jittered 8 x 13 grid instead of random positions, so it no longer clumps.

## Measured
- `hyperframes check`: passes. Kept warnings: pile.html length (the canvas), thumbnail concepts B and C dimmed on purpose (contrast), the 4 podcast camera crops share one photo.
- Upload file `renders/cutsway-reel-rawpile-30s-instagram.mp4`: H.264 yuv420p + AAC 48 kHz, -13.9 LUFS, peak -1.5 dBFS. Audio onsets measured on the 0.47 s beat grid.
- Not heard: the music and mix were checked by level meters, waveform and onset analysis only.

## Weakest moments
- Some hook sublines and "WE CUT IT." are on screen for about 1 s, under HyperFrames' reading floor. This is on purpose for pace.
- v1 scenes are kept in `v1/` for reference.

## v3 (2026-10-29): voiceover and more motion
- Voiceover: local Kokoro (`../tools/make_vo.py`), one line per scene, fitted to its window (speed 1.08 to 1.25), with an EQ, compression and saturation chain; the music is ducked about 7 dB under it (`assets/audio/music-bed.wav`). Voice about 8.5 LU above the bed.
- Motion: a film-wide `compositions/fx.html` layer (embers, kick glow, cut flashes, glitch slices on drops, speed streaks before whips) plus beat punches inside every scene.
- Not heard or transcribed: pronunciation (e.g. "VOD", "Cutsway") is unverified. To use a premium voice, replace synth() in make_vo.py.
